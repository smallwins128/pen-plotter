R04 Etch · control box front · GX openings and window in the model

Control front: GX plates and a small angled window

white: openings in the steel · orange: M3 rivnut holes (GX plates) and Ø3.4 bezel screws (window) · blue dashed: Ø22 socket nuts / window acrylic · red dashed: lid band

  • GX openings, now in the model: 6 mm of play round every nut (was 3). The endstop pair moved 6 mm right so the servo and endstop plates keep a gap. 7 M3 rivnuts in all: 3 for the steppers, 2 for the servo, 2 for the endstops.
  • Window, now in the model: 96 × 40 mm at mid-height, a trapezium: left end leaning 40° like the power front, right end square, R3, 9 mm bezel, 4 screws. Centred on the servo and endstop row. The bezel runs from x 42 to 169: 18 mm clear of the endstop plate, and well inside the corner rivets.
  • Behind it is the open front of the box. The driver and board sit further back, so the window shows mostly the wiring behind the GX sockets.
