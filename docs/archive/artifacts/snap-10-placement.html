Where the 10 mm clip holes go

new 10 × 10 clip hole · white circles: existing lid screws and rivets · dashed: body lips under the lid

1 · Control box lid top · 8 holes

Corners and edge middles on a 185 × 107.5 grid, inside the body's lips so the clip legs hang free. The side and front middles sit 32 mm in from the lid screws. Same grid as the power lid's stacking holes, so the two tops line up.

3 · Power box front · 10 holes

Two rows of 5 below the vents, at 30 and 95 mm up. The outer columns line up with the end vents.
