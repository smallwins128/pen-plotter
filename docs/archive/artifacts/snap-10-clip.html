Snap-10: one hole in the steel, push-fit clips for M3 or M4

Cross section, 1:1 in mm. Grey: 1 mm steel. Black: printed clip. Orange: heat-set insert and screw axis.

The deal: we cut one standard hole, a 10 × 10 square with R1 corners, wherever you want a future mount. Any clip we print later snaps into any of those holes.

  • Square, not round, so the clip can't spin when you tighten a screw.
  • Push in from outside and the four prongs flex in, then click behind the steel. Squeeze the barbs from inside to remove it.
  • M3 or M4 is a clip choice, not a hole choice. The same hole takes both; the M4 clip just has a taller head for the longer insert.
  • Heat-set insert in the head. If you don't have a soldering iron for inserts, a hex nut trap works in the same head.
  • Good for light things: cable clips, sensors, a small panel, a phone holder. Don't hang the plotter off one.

Placement rules I'd check for you: at least 5 mm from a bend, 3 mm from other cutouts, outside the 20 mm band under the lid lips, and 10 mm clear of anything inside.

Print: PETG if you have it, as it survives the snap better. Black PLA works for clips you fit once.
