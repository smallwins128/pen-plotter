Etch Power Front Windows
R04 Etch · power box front · experiment, not in the model

Power front windows: one long, or four angled

Both use a black PETG bezel outside, with clear 3 mm acrylic clamped between the bezel and the steel. Screws come from inside into heat-set inserts in the bezel, so no screw heads show. Top accessory row dropped. Bottom vent row moved down to 42 mm to give the window room.

A · One long window

black: bezel outline · blue dashed: acrylic 300 × 72 · white: steel cutout · orange: M3 holes

  • Window 270 × 42 mm (was 384 × 34). Acrylic 300 × 72. Bezel 306 × 78.
  • 6 screws: 2 top, 2 bottom, 1 each end.
  • 8 mm of plain steel between the bezel and the vents above, 6 mm to the vent row below.

B · Four angled windows, same footprint

black: bezel outline · blue dashed: acrylic · white: steel cutout · orange: M3 holes

  • 4 windows, each 44 × 42 mm, leaning 25°. About 5 mm of bare steel between bezels. Total span 280 mm, about the same as A.
  • Each has its own small acrylic and bezel, held by 2 screws on opposite corners. 8 screws in all.
  • The steel webs between the windows keep the face stiffer than one long cutout. Short bezels also fit any printer bed.
