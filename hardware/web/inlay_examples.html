The print. One black part per mark: a 1.2 mm backer plate a few mm bigger than the pattern, with the fills or pins rising from it. Plugs and pins are 0.15 mm smaller than their hole on each side. Stick it on from inside with VHB tape.

Rules kept. Each mark sits 14 mm or more from the folded edges and clear of the lid screws and corner rivets. All steel between cuts is at least 2 mm wide and joined to the panel.
