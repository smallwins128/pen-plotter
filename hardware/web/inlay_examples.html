Lissajous roundel

A 3 : 2 Lissajous figure inside a broken ring, like an oscilloscope trace. A quieter mark than lettering, and it ties to the lid's spirograph.

  • ring Ø76
  • slot 2.2
  • bridges 2.0 @ 16

How the inlay works. Print each plug 0.15 mm under the cut on every side, standing 1.0 mm tall (flush), on a 2 mm backer flange that runs 3 mm past the cut all round. Hold it with VHB tape or the screws already on that face. On the lid, the backer also seals the cuts against dust and stiffens the slotted area.

Rules every study keeps. Cuts stay 7 mm in from the folded edges, 13 mm from the lid's fixing holes, and out of the strip the lid's front lip covers. Steel webs are at least 1.5 mm. Turn on Keep-outs to see them.
