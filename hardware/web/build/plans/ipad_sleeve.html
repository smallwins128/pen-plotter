iPad sleeve

Two bottom clips and two side clips hold the iPad (7th gen, 10.2"; same body as the 8th gen) in landscape with its back flat on the SKÅDIS. Each clip has its own lugs (the hook's neck and lip), so there's no rail between the iPad and the board. Two bottom L corners carry it; one cap, centred on the top edge, stops it tipping out (replaces the two side clips: the left one had no purchase). The port is on the right.

drag · scroll / pinch

How it goes on

  • Clips: push the lugs through the slots, then let the clip drop about 6 mm. The lip hangs behind the board, below the slot.
  • Bottom clips first: they carry the iPad (two lugs each, both in the bar).
  • iPad: drop it into the bottom clips, back flat on the board.
  • Side clips: hang them like the bottom ones (push in 6 mm high, drop). The iPad slides down between them from the top, behind their lips, onto the bottom bars. Off: lift it straight up.
  • Checked in CAD: every lug on a real slot; no clash hanging the clips or dropping the iPad in; the side lips stop it moving forward.

Where it sits

  • The slots set the iPad's place: its short edges land 13 slot columns apart (260 mm), on columns that alternate 20 mm in height. So left and right clips share an outline but hide their lugs in different spots.
  • Left side clip v2: v1 had its lug at its top end, flush with its outer face, so nothing in front of the board clamped it and it had no purchase. v2 has a flange round the slot like the rail hook's: 3 mm past the slot's outer side and top, 6 mm below its floor, 1.7 mm on the inner side (the iPad covers the rest). It sits 16 mm higher than the right clip (the slot grid).
  • Top centre is clear of the buttons (the volume keys sit near the top-left).
