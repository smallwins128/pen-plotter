iPad sleeve

Three clips hold the iPad (7th gen, 10.2"; same body as the 8th gen) in landscape with its back flat on the SKÅDIS. Each clip has its own lugs (the hook's neck and lip), so there's no rail between the iPad and the board. Two bottom L corners carry it; one cap, centred on the top edge, stops it tipping out (replaces the two side clips: the left one had no purchase). The port is on the right.

drag · scroll / pinch

How it goes on

  • Clips: push the lugs through the slots, then let the clip drop about 6 mm. The lip hangs behind the board, below the slot.
  • Bottom clips first: they carry the iPad (two lugs each, both in the bar).
  • iPad: drop it into the bottom clips, back flat on the board.
  • Top cap last: push its lug into the slot just above the iPad's top edge, 7 mm high, then let it drop. The cap sits over the edge and its lip covers 3 mm of the bezel. Off: lift the cap 7 mm, pull it out, lift the iPad.
  • Checked in CAD: every lug on a real slot; no clash hanging the clips, dropping the iPad in, or hanging the cap with the iPad in; the iPad can't move forward or up.

Where it sits

  • The slots set the iPad's place: its short edges land 13 slot columns apart (260 mm), on columns that alternate 20 mm in height. So left and right clips share an outline but hide their lugs in different spots.
  • The cap's lug is in the column 10 mm left of the iPad's centre; the cap itself is centred. Its slot runs across the iPad's top edge, so the neck sits behind the iPad, inside the board.
  • Front contact: the lip grips the board's back below the slot, so the cap's back face presses the board's front beside the slot, up to the slot's top (15 mm; the hook's flange: 14). The v1 left side clip had none and tipped off.
  • Top centre is clear of the buttons (the volume keys sit near the top-left).
