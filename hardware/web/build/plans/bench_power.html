Bench power

One 6 A wall point feeds the bench. All mains equipment is bought ready-made; you only wire the low-voltage side. Lofi, not to scale for small parts. Bench, rack and socket positions come from the wall C plan.

6 A budget

A 6 A point gives about 1380 W at 230 V. Wattages are typical figures, not measured.

Rules

  • No heat gun on this strip. About 2000 W is well over 6 A. Run it from a 16 A point elsewhere.
  • Mains stays inside bought boxes: strip, brick, lab supply, charger. You only wire 12 V.
  • Put the smart plug on the 12 V brick only. Not on the iron or the lab supply.
  • Master switch off when you leave the room.
