Car rack

Your 300 × 35 × 6 wood strips as shelves for Hot Wheels, three cars each. Two printed brackets per strip hang on the SKÅDIS rail system; the strip just rests in their ledges. Four sliders drop on from above, one at each end and two between: each is a low ridge with tyre grooves, and each car sits in the dip between two of them. The strips aren't cut true, so nothing grips the wood: 1 mm or more of slack everywhere. Car sizes are estimates (≈ 75 × 32 × 35); measure one with the caliper.

Front view

Two strips, one hook row apart twice (80 mm). Brackets on hooks 160 apart, so the end sliders clear the brackets' front lips.

Bracket, side section

Ledge 38 inside (strip 35 + 1.5 slack each side); the front lip stands 3 mm over the strip top. The car width is an estimate.

One bay, from the front

Slider: a 30 mm ridge with 25° ramps (7 mm high, so a car's overhang clears it), tyre grooves 1.2 deep where the wheels sit (track ≈ 22, estimate), legs 4 mm down over the strip's front and back edges. 60 mm of flat between ridges.

How it goes up

  • Hooks in two slots of one row, 160 mm apart (4 hook spaces). Brackets slide down onto them.
  • Lay the strip in the ledges, then drop four sliders on: one flush with each end, two 90 mm in from them (centres at ±45 and ±135 from the middle).
  • The ledge's front lip stops the strip walking forward; the bracket plate stops it going back.
  • Next strip up: two hook rows higher (80 mm). That leaves ≈ 60 mm over a strip for a ≈ 35 mm car and your fingers.

Open questions

  • How many strips? Each takes 76 mm of height; a full 56 cm SKÅDIS fits 6 if nothing else is on it.
  • Measure one car (length, width, height) and your thickest and widest strip.
  • Cars side-on (as drawn) or nose-out? Nose-out fits 5–6 per strip at ≈ 35 mm pitch and needs no dividers, but a 35 mm deep strip leaves half the car hanging.
  • Sliders sit loose, held in place by the cars and the legs; if they wander, a dab of blu-tack under each fixes it.
