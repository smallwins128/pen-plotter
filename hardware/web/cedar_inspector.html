How it goes together

  • Rails to plates: each rail end sits on a printed tab. An M3 nut slides into the tab's channel from its free end, and an M3 × 6 pan head with a washer goes through whichever rail slot lands over the tab. Cut pieces can have their slots anywhere, and the channel still catches one.
  • Front rails: tab behind the web, screw from the front. Rear and base rails: tab inside the hat channel, screw from outside through the web.
  • Base rail: lies flat, web up, and the PSU sits on it. Only the bay end is screwed, so no screw head sits under the PSU.
  • PSU: its back face (fan, mains socket, switch) sits in the right plate's window, held by its own 4 screws through the corner pads.
  • Deck: 4 × M3 countersunk into heat-set inserts in the deck tabs. The board sits on 4 printed standoffs with inserts. The 24-pin cable rises through the slot at the back of the deck.
  • Left plate: 4 vent slots where the PSU likely pulls air in on its cable side.
