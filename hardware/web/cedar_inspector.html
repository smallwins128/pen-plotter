How it goes together

  • Wood: 6 slats (35 × 6, cut to 227), 4 under and 2 at the back, each in its own slot in both plates. Each one screws to the sleeve with an M3 × 10 into a drop-in nut. The PSU sits on bottom slats 1 and 2.
  • DIN rails: 3 (top, slope 1, slope 2). Each end sits in the standard seat; the clamp's 2 × M3 × 12 countersunk screws go into drop-in nuts slid in from the seat's free end. Each rail also screws into a rib on the sleeve.
  • Top rail: 9 KV121 and the DIN switch for the board's 2-pin socket, starting 30 mm in so your fingers fit behind the handle.
  • Handles: a 64 × 14 slot in each plate, 1 mm above the top rail's clamp, with 8 mm of plate above it.
  • Interlocks: the PSU's back end sits in a 2 mm pocket in the right plate, and its cable end in a 3 mm collar on the end wall. 3 keys on the end wall fit notches in the shell, and the shell sits in a 1.2 mm groove in the left plate.
  • End wall: a flat print, stopped by the shell's ledge and held there by the PSU. The ATX board sits on 2 bars across the PSU window, on 15 mm standoffs.
