Control lid: Posca doodle directions

Drawn to scale on the control lid top (457 × 301 mm, front edge at the bottom). Black = Posca, grey = bare brushed stainless. Dashed circles are the lid screws (Ø15 washers) and the 4 accessory holes; keep the pen 3 mm off them. Reshuffle for new layouts.

A · Cells

Rounded cells, line fills and solid blacks

  • Straight from your first reference: each cell a rounded shape, about a third solid black, the rest filled with bowed parallel lines.
  • Pens: PC-5M for the solids, PC-1MR (0.7) for lines. Lines about 3 mm apart read well at arm's length.
B · Doodle bomb

Packed characters round a bubble-letter ETCH

  • Your second reference. This is a layout guide only: blobs to turn into characters, sized so none is smaller than about 25 mm (easier with a Posca).
  • Draw outlines first with PC-3M, then fill the black shadows.
C · Hybrid

Cells that come alive

  • Direction A's cells, but a few of them get eyes and mouths. Calmer than B, more playful than A.

Posca on stainless

  • Scuff lightly with a grey Scotch-Brite pad, wipe with IPA, don't touch it after.
  • Posca is water-based: it wipes off bare steel until sealed. Finish with a clear spray (matte or satin), 2–3 light coats.
  • Pencil doesn't show on steel: sketch the big shapes with a white Posca or a fine dry-erase marker first.
  • Direction A is generative, so later the plotter can draw it with a Posca on a removable panel. On the lid itself, by hand.
