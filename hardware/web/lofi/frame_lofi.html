What changed from v3

  • Rails lifted 28 mm clear of the 560s (your "longer feet"). The bottom wheels run 6.2* above the 560, so the left limit drops from 67 to ≈ 48*: the leading wheel now stops at the end plate. The plate's bottom edge clears the table by 32* and the motor by 35* (were 3.6 and 7.5).
  • The rails hang on the end plates. Each rail end is held by 2 M5 into its tapped end, through 6 mm PETG. That's the part to watch in the inspector.
  • The 700 bar stays uncut. It passes through a 20.4 mm square window in each X plate, plus a 20 mm collar with M5 + T-nuts into the bar's slots. The X plates become PETG prints of the ACRO outline, 8 mm thick* (acrylic is 6).
  • Corner feet: each foot caps a LINNMON corner, with 20 mm lips down the front or back edge and the end. The frame sits 20 mm in from the front and back edges, which centres it.
  • Pen drop is now ≈ 137*. That's for the carriage redesign to handle, as agreed.

Need your call

  1. LINNMON top thickness: I've used 34* mm. Can you measure it? The lips need it.
  2. Gap: 28 mm between each rail and its 560 leaves the wheels 6.2* of clearance. OK, or do you want more?
  3. Rail hold: 2 M5 into each tapped rail end, with nothing under the rail, is the lean option. Want a rib on the end plate under the rail as well? It would sit inside the wheels' path at the very end.
  4. X plate thickness: 8 mm PETG, or keep it 6 like the acrylic?
