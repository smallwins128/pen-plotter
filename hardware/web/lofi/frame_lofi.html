What the sketch says

  • End plates go on the 1000 side: each one caps a rail end and bolts to the 560 below it. That's 4 plates, 4 × M5 each (stock M5).
  • The feet set the ride height. The ACRO plate hangs 11* below the frame's underside and the motor 8*. With 15 mm feet the plate clears the table by 3.6*. The feet aren't just broader, they have to be at least about 14 mm tall.
  • The 700 bar is too long: between the two plates' inner faces it's ≈ 562*. Cut your 700 or buy a new one. Neither needs anything else to change.
  • Left limit: the bar can't get closer than ≈ 67* to the left end, because the plate's bottom wheel meets the 560. The right side is the stop, wherever you set it.
  • Front to back over the motors: ≈ 642* on a 600 table, so they hang ≈ 21* past the edges if the frame is centred.
  • Pen drop: the bar's underside is ≈ 109* above the table. The Y carriage redesign has to reach down that far.

Need your call

  1. Motors past the table edge: the motors hang ≈ 21* past the front and back edges. OK? They can't go inside the plate: there they'd hit the bottom wheels. The wall behind needs the same room.
  2. Ends: the end plates make the frame 1012 long, so they stand 6 past each end of the 1000 top. The feet reach inward under the 560s, not outward. Is that OK?
  3. Feet: 15 mm tall, 90 × 60 footprint, plain PETG. Do you want rubber pads or screws into the table?
  4. Rail ends: are your 1000s tapped at the ends (M5)? If not, the plate takes T-nuts in the rail's side slots instead.
  5. Bar: cut the 700 to ≈ 562, or buy a new one?
  6. Y carriage: is the dumb plate going on your OpenBuilds 65.5 plate (3 wheels on the 2020)?
