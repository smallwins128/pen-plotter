iPad sleeve

Four clips hold the iPad (8th gen, 10.2") in landscape with its back flat on the SKÅDIS. Each clip has its own lugs (the hook's neck and lip), so there's no rail between the iPad and the board. Two side clips, two bottom L corners; the port is on the right.

drag · scroll / pinch

How it goes on

  • Clips: push the lugs through the slots, then let the clip drop about 6 mm. The lip hangs behind the board, below the slot.
  • Bottom clips first: they carry the iPad (two lugs each, both in the bar).
  • iPad: slide it down from the top, behind the side clips' lips, until it sits on the bottom bars. Off: lift it straight up.
  • Checked in CAD: every lug on a real slot, no clash while hanging the clips, none while the iPad drops in.

Where it sits

  • The slots set the iPad's place: its short edges land 13 slot columns apart (260 mm), on columns that alternate 20 mm in height. So left and right clips share an outline but hide their lugs in different spots.
  • Left side clip ends 34 mm below the top corner, to miss the top button (position guessed).
  • Right side clip starts 22 mm above the charging port.
