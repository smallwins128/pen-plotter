Why it sits where it does

  • Grab zone, bottom band: what you pick up seated, mid-job: knife, tape, pens. The caliper hangs just above them on the right, because every print and every cut starts with a measurement.
  • Driver strip, middle: one printed bar holds all the drivers and both hex keys, ordered from least used (tester, far left) to most used (precision driver, right).
  • Top: the ruler lies flat across the top on two cradles. It is long, used less, and lifts straight off.
  • Rarely used, bottom left: spanner and travel adapter sit farthest from your hand.
  • Open slot, bottom right: the best spot on the panel is left free on purpose.

Open questions

  • Flush cutter and wire stripper: wiring for Etch is daily work. Do they stay in the cupboard, or take the open slot?
  • Remote: which light does it switch? If it is the bench light, it moves to the open slot.
  • Toy car: does it go on the wall, as the shelf drawn top right, or not at all?
  • The SKÅDIS's height above the bench is from the 3D model, not measured.
