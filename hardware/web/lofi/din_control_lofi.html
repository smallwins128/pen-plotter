Control box · DIN rail lofi v3

Top view, front wall (GX sockets) at the bottom. TB6600s lie flat on L-clips with their terminals facing right. The board is rotated 180° from your photo and sits at the left end, near the USB panel. Duct A (back) carries power, USB and logic; duct B (front) carries motor and I/O wires to the GX sockets.

Wiring split

  • Duct A (back): 24 V in from the receiving panel, driver VCC/GND, board → driver PUL/DIR/ENA, USB-C and antenna to the left-wall panel.
  • Duct B (front): TB6600 A±/B± and board I/O (endstops, servo) → GX sockets.
  • Crossovers: only each driver's 2 power wires, which hop back over its motor terminals. The board's power now faces straight into duct A.
  • USB lead: leaves the board's front-right corner and runs along the left wall to the panel, in neither duct (about 200 mm). A right-angle USB-C plug helps.
  • Noise: 24 V DC next to USB and signals is fine; twist the power pairs and use a shielded USB cable. The risk is endstops next to the motor phases in duct B, so use twisted or shielded endstop cable and turn on debounce.

Placement (mm)

  • Rail: 370 long (x −175 to 195) on grid row z −2.5, with M4 screws at x −140, −20, 100, 180.
  • TB6600: 96 × 57 × 28 body on a 92 mm pitch (3 wall + 57 body + 12 terminals + 20 wire gap). Signal end at the back, VCC end at the front.
  • Board: rotated 180° (USB to the front, power to the back), at x −167 to −80, shifted 7.5 back from the rail, with 20 mm on its left for the I/O wires. PCB about 87 × 126 measured off your photo (unverified).
  • Free space: about 20 mm right of TB3 for motor wires, then the right wall. The receiving panel's jacks may stick in there (check). The board's I/O wires pass about 25 mm from the fan.

Clips

  • TB6600 L-clip: 3 mm base, 96 × 60, with a DIN foot under it. A 28 tall wall on the left takes the flange: 2 vertical slots 87 apart, running 8–20 mm up. Those slots cover the flange hole whether it sits 10 or 18 from the base, so the driver can go label up or label down.
  • Board clips ×2: flat bars about 130 long running front–back, with slots 118 apart (±3) on 6 mm bosses. They sit 78 apart along the rail and slide.
  • The U-slots in the TB6600 flange are open at the ends, so the screws can go into the clip first and the driver slides down onto them.
