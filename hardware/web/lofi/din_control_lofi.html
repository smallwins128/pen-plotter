Control box · DIN rail lofi v2

Top view, front wall (GX sockets) at the bottom. TB6600s lie flat on L-clips with their terminals facing right. The board sits upright, as in your photo. Duct A (back) carries power, USB and logic; duct B (front) carries motor and I/O wires to the GX sockets.

Wiring split

  • Duct A (back): 24 V in from the receiving panel, driver VCC/GND, board → driver PUL/DIR/ENA, USB-C and antenna to the left-wall panel.
  • Duct B (front): TB6600 A±/B± and board I/O (endstops, servo) → GX sockets.
  • Crossovers: each driver's 2 power wires hop back over its motor terminals. The board's power terminals face the front, so its 2 power wires run round the board's right side to duct A.

Placement (mm)

  • Rail: 380 long (x −195 to 185) on grid row z −2.5, with M4 screws at x −180, −60, 60, 140.
  • TB6600: 96 × 57 × 28 body on a 92 mm pitch (3 wall + 57 body + 12 terminals + 20 wire gap). Signal end at the back, VCC end at the front.
  • Board: upright as in your photo (USB to the back), shifted 10 back from the rail. PCB about 87 × 126 measured off your photo (unverified).
  • Free space: 46 mm to the right of the board for I/O wiring. TB1 sits 5 mm from the fan.

Clips

  • TB6600 L-clip: 3 mm base, 96 × 60, with a DIN foot under it. A 28 tall wall on the left takes the flange: 2 vertical slots 87 apart, running 8–20 mm up. Those slots cover the flange hole whether it sits 10 or 18 from the base, so the driver can go label up or label down.
  • Board clips ×2: flat bars about 130 long running front–back, with slots 118 apart (±3) on 6 mm bosses. They sit 78 apart along the rail and slide.
  • The U-slots in the TB6600 flange are open at the ends, so the screws can go into the clip first and the driver slides down onto them.
