Wire bend jig

Spinning sleeves on M3 posts, on a Meccano plate (12.7 mm grid). Shown with a sample bend layout and a wire. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom.

Sleeves

  • Ø16 signal wire, Ø24 18 AWG, Ø40 multi-core cable. Each 15.5 tall with a 3 mm lip.
  • M3 × 16 screw up through the plate, nut snug on top; the sleeve drops over and spins on it.
  • Print lip down, no supports. 4 × Ø16, 4 × Ø24, 2 × Ø40.

Using it

  • Set posts where the bends go, pull the wire round them, tie, lift off.
  • Bend radius = half the sleeve: about 5 × the wire's outside diameter (rule of thumb).
