Side tower

Your slotted-angle rack rebuilt as 60 × 60 × 152 cm, boxed in with two 12 mm ply sides, a ply shelf and a ply top. It replaces the folding table and rack A at the bench end. Lofi: sizes in mm, shelf heights to be set when the frame is built.

How it looks

On wall C

Front view to scale. The tower sits 40 mm right of the bench, its front in line with the bench front.

What it gives you

  • Two big bays, about 600 × 600 × 740 each: room for SAMLA 22 L and 11 L boxes side by side, or the lab supply and printer filament.
  • A top at about 1530, around chin height for you: a place for things you look at, not dig into. A plant, a printed part on show, the overhead camera.
  • The left side faces the bench. It is a second 600 × 1524 board inside your reach: hooks, the hole grid, the screwdriver rack, cables.
  • Rigid. The ply sides bolted to the posts stop the slotted angle from wobbling. Add one L-bracket from the top into the wall.

Check before cutting

  • Switch gap: the tower's right side ends about 2664 from the window corner; the switch panel was estimated at 2680. Measure. If it is tight, slide the tower left and close the 40 mm gap to the bench.
  • Frame size: build the 60 × 60 frame first and give the shop measured sizes. The shelf drops inside the frame, so it is a few mm under the inside size.
  • Top between the sides: a top laid over the side panels would need about 624, more than the 610 the sheet gives.
