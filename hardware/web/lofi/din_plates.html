DIN adapter plates

Black PETG plates between your bought DIN clips (blue ghost) and the parts (grey ghost). Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom.

TB6600 plate (×3)

  • 65 × 100 base, 4 thick, with a window in the middle. About 24 cm³.
  • One clip under the driver's middle. Flat-head M3 screws go up from the clip's rail side into nuts sunk in the base top.
  • Two posts on the left take the flange. The slot runs 8–20 mm up, so the driver can sit label up or label down.
  • The M3 nut drops into a channel on the post's outside; the screw goes through the flange's open U-slot into it.

Board plate (×1)

  • An H-frame, 90 × 132 × 4, with two clips 60 apart. About 34 cm³.
  • 4 bosses, 6 tall, at 78 × 118. Each has a Ø6 float hole, so there's about ±1.3 mm of play for your measurement.
  • M3 from the top, with a washer and nut underneath. Tighten before clipping onto the rail.
  • The board centre sits 10 behind the rail, as in the lofi.

  • Height from the rail's base to the clip's top face when it's clipped on. My guess is about 16 (unverified).
