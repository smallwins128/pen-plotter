DIN grip test coupon

The 0.2 mm coupon (black PETG) on a short piece of 35 × 7.5 DIN rail. It slides on from the rail's end. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom.

What you're seeing

  • 3 mm top resting on the rail's two edges.
  • A hook down each side that reaches 2.2 mm under each 1 mm edge. Nothing flexes.
  • "Look down the rail end" moves the coupon to the rail's end so you can see the hooks under the edges.
  • Three coupons to print: 0.1, 0.2 and 0.3 mm clearance. Each prints standing on its end.

On the real plates

  • This profile is built into the underside of each TB6600 and board plate.
  • Plates slide onto the rail on the bench; the rail is then screwed down through the gaps between them.
