DIN grip test coupon

The loose 0.3 mm grip with its anchor tab (black PETG) on a short piece of 35 × 7.5 DIN rail. It slides on from the rail's end. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom.

What you're seeing

  • 3 mm top resting on the rail's two edges; 0.3 mm play so it slides freely.
  • Anchor: M3 × 12 screw into a brass M3 × 4 insert in the tab. Its tip jacks on the rail floor, so tightening pulls the hooks up against the edges: locked, no rattle.
  • A hook down each side that reaches 2.2 mm under each 1 mm edge. Nothing flexes.
  • "Look down the rail end" moves the coupon to the rail's end so you can see the hooks under the edges.
  • Print it lying on its top face (hooks up). No supports.

On the real plates

  • This profile is built into the underside of each TB6600 and board plate.
  • Plates slide onto the rail on the bench; the rail is then screwed down through the gaps between them. Each plate's anchor tab sits in the gap beside it, reachable from above.
