SKÅDIS rail

One hook type on the board; every holder is a plate that drops onto the hooks. The hook pushes into a slot and drops 7 mm; its front is a dovetail rail. Each plate has a sleeve per hook: a dovetail groove, open at the bottom, with a roof it rests on. Below: five generic plates, the fit strip (sleeves at 0.15, 0.25 and 0.35 mm clearance) and the hook.

How it goes on

  • Hook: lip and neck through a slot, push down 7 mm. The lip now sits behind the board, 6 mm below the slot.
  • Plate: hold it just above the hooks, lower it; the sleeve's flared mouth finds the rail, the plate slides down and sits on the sleeve roof.
  • Foot: the plate's bottom edge reaches back to the board, so a tall plate can't flex in under a heavy tool. It is notched at each sleeve, and the sleeve runs down to the bottom edge, so the hook slides straight through. Checked in CAD: no clash anywhere along the slide, every plate.
  • Off: lift the plate straight up. Hooks stay on the board.

Grid rule

  • Hooks in one row of slots, so sleeves sit on a 40 mm pitch.
  • Plate widths 36 / 76 / 116 … (40 n − 4), leaving 4 mm between plates.
  • One sleeve holds a small plate (the 26 mm rail stops it turning); two for anything wider.
