What the file says

  • Gantry plate wheels sit 70 apart along the rail. The slot sets the cross-rail spacing from 21.6 to 39.1, which fits a 20 mm face. A second hole at 59.25 fits a 40 mm face.
  • NEMA17 on the gantry plate: 31 square, Ø32 boss. The X motor rides on the plate (ACRO style).
  • The carriage plate's wheels are fixed at 59.25, which fits a 2040 bar. Ours is a 2020.

What it does to the plot

  • Along X ≈ 461* (A3 needs 450: +11). The plate is 93.9 long against 65.5 today.
  • Along the bar ≈ 338* (A3 needs 330: +8). The carriage can't pass over a gantry plate.
  • Bar underside 7.1* above the rails, against the 15 placeholder in params.
  • Motors overhang the frame by about 48, front and back. That's inside the bar's own 50 overhang, so it needs no new space if the bar already clears the wall.

Your call

  1. Bar: change to a 700 × 2040 to suit the carriage plate (stiffer too), or keep the 2020 and print a carriage with ~39 mm wheel spacing?
  2. X motors: move them onto the plates (ACRO style, belt anchored at the rail ends), or keep them where they are today?
  3. Do you have the wheels, eccentric spacers and 6 mm spacers, or only the plates?
