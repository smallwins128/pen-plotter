Holes and cables

  • Cable slot: keep the bench 30 mm off the board. Every cable from the bench drops down that gap to the power below. No holes needed.
  • Two 35 mm grommets just above the power band, one each side of the SKÅDIS. Cables go behind the board there and run in the gap the clips leave.
  • One 10 mm hole beside the light shelf for the 12 V lead, so the light wire stays hidden.
  • 32 mm grid zones (dotted): beside and above the bins and around the solder zone. Drill on demand through the offcut jig; paint is on now, so tape each spot first.
  • Keep holes clear of the clip rows on the back (two rows, marked ⊓).
  • Skirting: the board stands in front of it, so the wall-side clips need packing at least as thick as the skirting, or the board leans out at the top. Measure the skirting depth.

What sits where

  • Hidden zone (0 to 700, behind the bench): 12 V brick, cable tie-down row, spare cable loops. Out of sight and out of the way.
  • Power band (770 to 900): main strip with master switch, PD charger, fuse block and two DC jacks.
  • Bins (940 to 1105): two hook-lip rails of six, smallest sizes left. Low, because you lift and tip them out: they need the easiest reach.
  • SKÅDIS (1140 to 1700): hand tools on hooks, which are easy to grab by the handle. Daily tools on the lower half, less-used ones higher.
  • Solder bits: magnetic bar and a solder and tape dowel on the SKÅDIS. Fume fan stands on the bench.
  • Tape test before drilling: sit at the bench and reach to the wall; tape where your grip stays comfortable. Stand and do the same. Shift the zones to your two tape marks.
  • Light shelf: one freed panel strip (912 × 150) as a shelf, the 12 V Beacon bar under its front edge.
