Holes and cables

  • Cable slot: keep the bench 30 mm off the board. Every cable from the bench drops down that gap to the power below. No holes needed.
  • Two 35 mm grommets just above the power band, one each side of the SKÅDIS. Cables go behind the board there and run in the gap the clips leave.
  • One 10 mm hole beside the light shelf for the 12 V lead, so the light wire stays hidden.
  • 32 mm grid zones (dotted): beside and above the bins and around the solder zone. Drill on demand through the offcut jig; paint is on now, so tape each spot first.
  • Keep holes clear of the clip rows on the back (two rows, marked ⊓ on the drawings).

What sits where

  • Power band (bottom 130): main strip with master switch, PD charger, fuse block and two DC jacks.
  • Bins: 3 rows of 4, smallest sizes left, as on the labels.
  • SKÅDIS: the hands zone. Pliers, cutters, strippers, tweezers, screwdrivers.
  • Solder zone: solder reel and wick on a dowel, flux, magnetic bar for small steel tools. Fume fan stands on the bench below it.
  • Light shelf: one freed panel strip (912 × 150) as a shelf, the 12 V Beacon bar under its front edge.
