Layers, front to back

How it holds together

  • All inserts are in L4 (the spacer): .
  • Back stack: from the back. Four pass through L1, L2, L3 and the fan's own corner holes; two hold the chin.
  • Front stack: from the front of L5 into L4. The face hides them.
  • Face: no screws. A 3 mm lip drops into the pad pocket, and 4 Ø6 × 2 magnets in its back pull on the four steel L5 screw heads (use zinc-plated steel screws, not stainless). Pull the face off to swap the pad.
  • Handle: 2 × M4 countersunk screws, driven up from inside the fan pocket before the fan goes in. The heads sit flush with the pocket roof.
  • Pads: cut each one 80 mm square and snip 16 mm off every corner, which clears the screws.
  • Wiring: the fan lead drops into the hollow chin through a notch in L3, then runs to the jack and switch on L4's right side.

Measure before printing

  • Handle: hole spacing, thread size, foot size64 mm and M4 assumed
  • Carbon pad: thickness10 assumed
  • Fan: is it 12 V or 24 V? The adaptor tops out at 12 V.
  • DC jack: panel hole sizeØ8 assumed
  • Switch: type and hole sizeØ6 toggle assumed
  • Heat-set insert length≤ 6 assumed; two meet back to back in L4
